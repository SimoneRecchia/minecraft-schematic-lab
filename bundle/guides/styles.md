# Style guides

Each style: proportions and massing · roof · signature details · palette (primary / secondary / accent / roof / trim). Check the target version (read_guide("versions")) — substitute missing blocks.

## Medieval
Stone ground floor + timber-framed upper floors, each upper floor jettied out by 1 on upside-down-stair corbels; plans ~1:1.5, 2–3 floors, dense clusters, crooked additions. Roof 45–63° gable or cross-gable, overhang 1–2, eaves trim. Details: corbels, shutters (open trapdoors), flower boxes, chimneys, hanging lanterns, wooden signs.
Palette: `cobblestone`+`stone_bricks`+`andesite` base / `white_terracotta`·`calcite` infill / `spruce_trapdoor`, red banners / `spruce_stairs` or `dark_oak_stairs` / `stripped_dark_oak_log` or `stripped_spruce_log` frame.

## Fantasy / fairy-tale
Exaggerated everything: steep tall roofs (1.5× the walls), bulging forms, crooked towers, asymmetry, round windows. Roofs: witch-hat cones, steep gables, colourful tiles, curved `profile`s. Details: bent spires, dormers everywhere, overgrowth, glowing windows, oversized chimneys.
Palette: `white_terracotta` / `stripped_dark_oak_log` / `amethyst_block`, flowers / `deepslate_tile_stairs`, `dark_prismarine_stairs`, `warped_stairs`, `crimson_stairs` / `mossy_cobblestone` base.

## Gothic cathedral
Cross (Latin) plan; nave 2–3× as tall as wide; repeated bays 5–7 wide; west façade with twin towers ≥1.5× the nave; transept + crossing tower or spire. Steep 2:1 slate roofs, needle spires (cone pitch 3–4). Details: flying buttresses (stepped pier + stair-arch flyer), pointed lancets (`arch` style `pointed`), rose window (stained-glass circle with wall tracery — script), pinnacles (walls + `end_rod`), portal with stepped pointed arches.
Palette: `deepslate_bricks`+`tuff_bricks` (or `stone_bricks`+`andesite`) / `polished_deepslate` / `purple`+`blue_stained_glass`, gold / `deepslate_tile_stairs` / `chiseled_deepslate`, `polished_blackstone_wall`.

## Castle / fortress
Hierarchy keep > gatehouse > corner towers > curtain walls; battered (sloped) bases; towers every 15–30 along the walls projecting half their diameter; curtain walls 3–5 thick, 10–20 high, 2–3-wide wall-walk with crenels (2-on/1-off on big walls). Roofs: flat crenellated tops or cone roofs on towers. Details: machicolations (ring of upside-down stairs under a projecting parapet), arrow slits 1×2–3, portcullis of `iron_bars`, banners, a moat.
Palette: `stone_bricks` (+cracked/mossy) / `andesite`, `cobbled_deepslate` base / banners / `dark_oak_stairs` or `deepslate_tile_stairs` / `polished_andesite`, `stone_brick_wall`.

## Japanese
Raised stone plinth, strong horizontals, deep eaves (2–3), post-and-beam grid with posts every 2–3 blocks; engawa veranda around the house. Irimoya (hip + gable) with a concave sweep and upturned corners (`profile` `[0.5,0.5,1,1,2]`, overhang 2–3). Details: shoji (white panels framed by stripped logs / birch trapdoors), torii, stone lanterns, gravel gardens, cherry trees, koi pond.
Palette: `white_concrete`·`calcite` / `stripped_dark_oak_log`, `spruce_planks` / `crimson_planks` or `stripped_mangrove_log` (red), `cherry_leaves` / `deepslate_tile_stairs`, `dark_prismarine_stairs` / `smooth_stone`, `polished_andesite` plinth.

## Chinese
Platform podium, red columns, symmetric courtyard compounds, tiered roofs. Strongly concave roofs with sharply upturned corners, multiple eaves (double-eave halls). Details: dougong brackets (stepped stairs under the eaves), gold ridge ornaments, white marble balustrades, moon gates (round `arch`).
Palette: `red_terracotta`·`stripped_mangrove_log` columns / `white_concrete` / `gold_block`, `cyan`+`green_terracotta` brackets / `deepslate_tile_stairs` (grey) or `bamboo_mosaic_stairs` (imperial yellow) / `quartz_block`, `diorite_wall` balustrade.

## Nordic / viking
Long hall 30–50 × 12–15, low walls (3–4), huge roof (45–50°+, overhang 2–3), crossed gable boards. Details: dragon-head finials, sod/moss roofs, log ridge beam, carved posts, shields on walls, braziers.
Palette: `spruce_log`, `stripped_spruce_log` / `dark_oak_planks` / red wool, lanterns / `spruce_stairs`, `dark_oak_stairs`, `moss_block` / `cobbled_deepslate`, `blackstone` base.

## Desert / Arabic
Thick cubic masses, flat roofs with parapets, courtyards, small high windows, domes and minarets. Details: onion domes, horseshoe and pointed arches, mashrabiya screens (trapdoor lattice), glazed tile bands, palm trees.
Palette: `smooth_sandstone`, `sandstone` / `cut_sandstone`, `packed_mud` / `cyan`+`light_blue_terracotta`, `*_glazed_terracotta`, gold / domes in `waxed_oxidized_cut_copper` or `white_concrete` / `stripped_jungle_log`, `chiseled_sandstone`.

## Mediterranean
White stepped cubes cascading down a hill, arches, pergolas, terraces. Low 1:2 terracotta roofs (pitch 0.5) or flat. Details: blue shutters, vines, pots, arcades, cypress trees.
Palette: `white_concrete`, `calcite`, `smooth_quartz` / `stripped_oak_log` / `warped_trapdoor`, `light_blue_terracotta` / `granite_stairs`, `brick_stairs`, `acacia_stairs` / `diorite_wall`.

## Modern / contemporary
Horizontal slabs, cantilevers, big glazing, 1–2 materials, flat thin roofs with 1–2 overhang. Details: floor-to-ceiling glass in `black_concrete` frames, wood cladding panels, pools, hidden lighting, minimal landscaping.
Palette: `white_concrete`, `smooth_quartz` / `light_gray_concrete`, `gray_concrete`, `smooth_stone` / `stripped_oak_log`, `spruce_planks` / `smooth_stone_slab` / `black_concrete`, `tinted_glass`, `glass`.

## Steampunk
Industrial Victorian: brick bodies, steep mansards, copper domes, chimneys and pipes, asymmetric machinery. Details: pipes (walls, iron bars, lightning rods), gears (`grindstone`), vents (`iron_trapdoor`), smokestacks with campfires, catwalks.
Palette: `bricks` / `cut_copper` in mixed oxidation / gold, `lightning_rod` / `waxed_*_cut_copper_stairs`, `dark_oak_stairs` / `iron_bars`, `chain`, `copper_grate`, `polished_blackstone`.

## Elven / organic
Vertical, slender, curving, integrated with giant trees, few right angles. Swooping leaf-like spires, arches, bridges between trees, vines and glow.
Palette: `stripped_birch_log`, `calcite` / `birch_planks`, `pale_oak_planks` / gold, `amethyst_block`, `end_rod` / `oxidized_cut_copper_stairs`, `prismarine_stairs`, `warped_stairs` / `flowering_azalea_leaves`, `glow_lichen`.

## Dwarven / underground
Monumental, angular, symmetric, carved into rock; 3×3+ pillars, vaulted halls, huge doors, statues, lava channels behind bars, geometric patterns (`layers`).
Palette: `deepslate_bricks`, `polished_deepslate` / `deepslate_tiles`, `polished_blackstone_bricks` / `gilded_blackstone`, `gold_block`, `raw_gold_block`, copper / `polished_basalt` columns / `chiseled_deepslate`, `lava`+`iron_bars`, `shroomlight`.

## Rustic farm / cottage
Low, cosy, 1–1.5 floors, roof reaching almost to the ground, lean-tos, gardens. Steep thatch or plank gables, hips. Details: fences, haystacks, barrels, crooked chimney, vegetable patches, flowers.
Palette: `oak_log` / `oak_planks`, `cobblestone`, `mossy_cobblestone` / flowers, `barrel` / `hay_block` + `bamboo_mosaic_stairs` or `spruce_stairs` / `stripped_oak_log`.

## Ruins / overgrown
Broken silhouettes: collapsed roofs, jagged wall tops stepping down 1–3 irregularly (script with noise), rubble piles, moss gradient rising from the ground, trees growing through.
Palette: `mossy_stone_bricks`, `cracked_stone_bricks` / `cobblestone`, `mossy_cobblestone`, `andesite`, `gravel` / `moss_block`, `moss_carpet`, `vine`, `glow_lichen`, `cobweb` / — / `rooted_dirt`, `hanging_roots`, `coarse_dirt`.

## Futuristic / sci-fi
Clean curves, modular panels, huge scale, glowing lines, greebles. Domes and angled shells.
Palette: `white_concrete`, `smooth_quartz` / `light_gray_concrete`, `iron_block`, `polished_andesite` / `cyan`,`light_blue_concrete`, `light_blue_stained_glass` / `smooth_quartz_slab` / `black_concrete`, `polished_deepslate`, `tinted_glass`; light strips `end_rod`, `sea_lantern`; greebles `observer`, `daylight_detector`, `iron_trapdoor`, `lightning_rod`.
