## What you can do

- Say that your project is based on, a fork of, or compatible with VibeSchem.
- Name VibeSchem when you write, talk or make videos about it.
- Build, run and share the unmodified project as published here, under its own name.

## What needs permission

- Publishing, listing or distributing a changed version or a fork under the name VibeSchem, a
  confusingly similar name, or with the VibeSchem logo.
- Suggesting that your project is the official VibeSchem, or endorsed by its author.

## Forking

Before you publish a fork, give it its own name and icon: `name` and `displayName` in
`plugin/.claude-plugin/plugin.json`, the entries in `.claude-plugin/marketplace.json`, `name` and
`display_name` in `mcpb/manifest.json`, the viewer title (`apps/web/index.html`, `apps/web/src/i18n.ts`)
and `mcpb/icon.svg`.

To ask for permission, open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/SimoneRecchia/minecraft-schematic-lab/issues).
